Our community guidelines

Good work starts
with how we work.

A shared environment works when everyone helps make it welcoming, useful, and trustworthy.

Talk to the society team
01

Make room for others

Ask questions, welcome beginners, and respect different backgrounds. Disagree with ideas thoughtfully.

02

Give credit generously

Acknowledge collaborators and cite sources. Share what you learned and be clear about what is your own work.

03

Treat data with care

Use data responsibly. Get permission where needed and keep personal information out of public projects and reports.

04

Follow through together

Communicate your commitments, share progress, and ask for help early. Bring concerns to the society team.

A good place to begin.

Who can join?

Students from any discipline are welcome. You don’t need prior experience in data science.

What is the difference between a student and a member?

A student account gives you learning resources, bookmarks, and event registration. Approved society members also access contribution opportunities, submit their work for review, and take part in society projects.

Can I join from anywhere in Pakistan?

Yes. Check individual events for online access or campus locations.

How do I become an ambassador?

Submit a campus ambassador application. The team reviews your plan and shares its decision through your account.
